Born at the Kitchen Table

A parent's answer to educational games that kids actually want to play.

As a parent of two young kids in Ontario, I did what most parents do — I tried the popular educational apps. They all looked great on paper. Curriculum-aligned, millions of users, glowing reviews.

But my kids didn't come back for a second try.

The games felt like homework dressed up in bright colours. The questions felt like quizzes, not adventures. My kids weren't learning because they weren't playing — and they weren't playing because nobody built these platforms with kids in the room.

So I started building the platform I wished existed. One where my own kids would actually ask to play.

Where learning feels like a game because it is a game — not a worksheet with a cartoon slapped on top. Where a fire truck rolls in when you help the right community helper, and a crab mascot cheers you on when you get it right.

Every game on Crabcake is tested by the toughest critics I know: my kids. If they don't want to play it again, it doesn't ship.

What makes Crabcake different

Crabcake is built by a parent, for kids. Every game is aligned to the Ontario, Canada Ministry of Education curriculum — covering Mathematics, Language, Science & Technology, and Social Studies from Kindergarten through Grade 4. But curriculum alignment is just the starting point. The real goal is making kids want to learn without being told to.

100% Kid Safe

Crabcake is completely free to play. There are no accounts to create, no personal information collected, and no external tracking. Everything is stored locally on your device and never leaves it. Your child's safety isn't a feature — it's the foundation.

Built in Ontario

Crabcake is proudly designed and built in Ontario, Canada. It's a one-person project driven by the belief that kids deserve better than what's out there — and that a parent who knows what's missing might be the right person to build it.
